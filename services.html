Planning your lessons

A Few Useful Details

Can I enquire for more than one service?

Yes. Choose the main service in the enquiry form and mention any additional services in your requirements. We can discuss each need separately.

Can I request tuition for only one subject?

Yes. Share the subject, class, board and topics you need help with. You can also enquire about a combination of subjects.

How are fees and lesson schedules decided?

Fees and schedules depend on the service, learner’s level, location, lesson frequency and teacher requirements. Discuss these details before starting.

Can a demo lesson be arranged?

Ask about a demo when making your enquiry. We will confirm the available arrangements for your requested service and teacher.

What should I include in a shadow teacher enquiry?

Explain the educational support required, the child’s class, locality, school expectations and preferred schedule. You can discuss further details directly with us rather than entering sensitive records in the enquiry form.
