Questions & answers

Before you get started.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Delhi NCR and mainly operate in Dwarka, Tilak Nagar, Janakpuri, Rohini, Noida, Paschim Vihar and Punjabi Bagh. Contact us to confirm availability for your locality and service.

Which classes and learning services can I enquire about?

You can enquire about school home tuition for Classes 1–12, spoken English, music lessons and shadow teacher support. Share your class, subjects and learning needs.

How do I request a teacher or demo lesson?

Use our enquiry form, call +91 9213723510, or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll discuss teacher availability and whether a demo can be arranged.

How much does tuition cost?

Fees depend on the subject or service, learner’s level, location, schedule and teacher requirements. Share your details to discuss fees before committing.

What does a shadow teacher help with?

A shadow teacher provides individual educational support, such as help with participation, learning tasks and routines. The role should be agreed with the family and school based on the child’s needs.

How can teachers register?

Visit the teacher registration section and share your contact details, subjects, qualifications, experience and preferred locations.
