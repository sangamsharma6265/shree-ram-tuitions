Questions & answers

Before you get started

Here are a few details to help you choose the right support.

What learning services can I enquire about?

You can enquire about home tuition for Classes 1–12, spoken English lessons, music teachers and shadow teacher support. Tell us your specific requirements so we can discuss suitable options.

How do I request a teacher or a demo lesson?

Use our contact form, call +91 9213723510, or message us on WhatsApp. Share the service you need, your locality and preferred timings. We will discuss availability and demo arrangements with you.

How much do lessons cost?

Fees depend on the service, learner’s level, location, schedule and teacher requirements. Contact us with these details to discuss the applicable fees before you commit.

Can I enquire about a particular musical instrument?

Yes. Mention the instrument or vocal training you are interested in, your current level and your locality. We will confirm whether a suitable teacher is available.

What does a shadow teacher help with?

A shadow teacher provides individual educational support, which may include help with classroom participation, learning tasks and daily learning routines. The specific role should be discussed with the family and school based on the child’s needs.

How can teachers register with Shree Ram Tuitions?

Visit the teacher registration section on our contact page. Share your subjects or specialisation, experience, preferred locations and contact details so we can review your profile.
