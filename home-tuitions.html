Questions & answers

Home tuition enquiries, explained.

Can I enquire about tuition for just one subject?

Yes. Share the subject, class, board and topics needing attention. You can also discuss support for a combination of subjects.

Do you cover my locality?

We cover Delhi NCR and mainly operate in Dwarka, Tilak Nagar, Janakpuri, Rohini, Noida, Paschim Vihar and Punjabi Bagh. Contact us with your locality to confirm teacher availability.

Can a demo lesson be arranged?

Ask about demo arrangements when sending your enquiry. Availability and any applicable terms will be discussed before a lesson is confirmed.

What are the tuition fees?

Fees depend on the class, subjects, location, lesson frequency and teacher requirements. Share these details to discuss the applicable fees.

Can lessons fit around school timings?

Share your preferred days and times. We’ll discuss whether a suitable teacher is available for that schedule.

Do you offer other learning services?

You can also enquire about spoken English, music lessons and shadow teacher support. Visit our services page to explore them.
